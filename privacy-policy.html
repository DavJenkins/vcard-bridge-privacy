Information handled by the app

vCard Bridge processes vCard text supplied by the user in either of these ways:

  • Text copied to the device clipboard and detected when vCard Bridge is opened
  • Compatible text shared directly to vCard Bridge through Android's sharing system

The app examines this text to identify valid vCard data and determine how many vCards it contains.

On-device processing

Normal vCard and contact processing occurs locally on the user's device. vCard Bridge does not automatically transmit this data to DEJ Software™. Optional diagnostic sharing is described below.

When the user selects Import, vCard Bridge provides the validated vCard data to the contacts application chosen by the user. Handling of that data after it is transferred is governed by the chosen contacts application and its privacy practices.

vCard Bridge does not read the user's existing contacts.

Advertising and analytics

vCard Bridge does not contain advertising and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Purchases and trial status

Direct Share is available through a free trial and may require an in-app purchase afterward.

Purchases are processed through Google Play. DEJ Software™ does not receive or store the user's payment-card information. Google Play may provide the app with purchase-status information needed to determine whether a feature has been unlocked.

The app may store trial and feature-access status locally on the device.

Data sharing

DEJ Software™ does not sell user information.

When the user initiates an import, vCard data is passed to the selected contacts application. This workflow remains local; any further handling depends on that application's behavior and privacy practices. Purchase processing and purchase-status verification are handled through Google Play.

Optional diagnostic reports

Diagnostic reporting is optional and initiated by the user. Before sharing, the user controls the diagnostic and privacy selections. Depending on those selections, a report may contain vCard or contact-related data, including raw data. Reports are not sent automatically: the user explicitly starts the email or share handoff and completes sending in the chosen application.

Diagnostic encryption is enabled by default. When enabled, the report is OpenPGP encrypted for DEJ Software™ before the email handoff. If encryption fails, that handoff is blocked; the app does not silently substitute plaintext. The user can explicitly disable encryption or choose Share another way to share the report as plaintext.

Information collected by DEJ Software™

DEJ Software™ does not require users to create an account. The app does not automatically send names, email addresses, contact lists, vCard contents, clipboard contents, or imported contact information to DEJ Software™. Users may choose to include information in a diagnostic report.

If a user voluntarily contacts DEJ Software™ by email, the information included in that correspondence will be used only to respond to the inquiry and provide support.

Data retention and deletion

Normal on-device processing does not send vCard or contact data to DEJ Software™ for retention. Information voluntarily sent by email, including diagnostic reports, is handled as support correspondence as described above.

App-specific information stored locally can be removed through the device's application settings or by uninstalling vCard Bridge. Contacts already imported into a contacts application must be managed or deleted through that contacts application.

Purchase records maintained by Google are governed by Google's policies.

Security

vCard Bridge keeps normal vCard processing on the device. Data is handed to another application only when the user initiates an import or diagnostic sharing. Diagnostic encryption and its explicit plaintext options are described above. No method of software operation can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or information-handling practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
